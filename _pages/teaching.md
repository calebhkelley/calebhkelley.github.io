## Teaching Philosophy

My pedagogical approach is grounded in usage-based theories of second language acquisition, drawing from cognitive-functional linguistics and complex dynamic systems theory. I view language learning as an emergent process where learners construct linguistic knowledge through meaningful interaction with authentic input, developing both form-meaning mappings and sociolinguistic competence.

### **Theoretical Foundation:**

**Usage-Based Learning.** Following cognitive-functional principles, I emphasize that language acquisition occurs through exposure to frequent, meaningful patterns in authentic contexts. Students develop grammatical knowledge inductively through repeated encounters with constructions in communicative situations.

**Embodied Cognition.** Language learning engages multiple cognitive systems. I incorporate multimodal input, gesture, and embodied activities that activate diverse neural pathways and strengthen form-meaning associations.

**Dynamic Systems Approach.** Language development is non-linear and emerges from complex interactions between learner, input, and social context. I create learning environments that support individual developmental trajectories while fostering collaborative meaning-making.

**Social Constructivism.** Knowledge construction occurs through social interaction. Students engage in authentic communicative tasks where linguistic forms emerge naturally from functional needs, mirroring first language acquisition processes.

### **Pedagogical Applications:**

**Input-Rich Environments.** High-frequency exposure to comprehensible, authentic materials allows students to extract patterns and develop implicit linguistic knowledge alongside explicit instruction.

**Form-Function Integration.** Grammatical structures are taught in meaningful contexts where their communicative function is transparent, supporting cognitive-functional mapping processes.

**Collaborative Meaning-Making.** Peer interaction and negotiation of meaning provide opportunities for hypothesis testing and linguistic accommodation within learners' zones of proximal development.

**Inclusive Identity Construction.** - Recognizing language as a tool for identity performance, I create spaces where diverse linguistic and cultural identities are valued, particularly supporting LGBTQ+ learners and other marginalized communities.
